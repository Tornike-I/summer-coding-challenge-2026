Paradox Loop

The room needs more hands than you have. So borrow your own past — rewind, and yesterday’s you walks beside you.

How to play

Step 1

Walk the room

→

One key press is one turn. Move one tile, or wait. Reach the exit and the level is done.

Step 2

Plates open doors

→

A door stays open only while a body stands on a plate of its shape. Step off and it shuts again.

Step 3

Rewind for a ghost

+

Rewind and the room resets — but a ghost now replays everything you just did, one turn at a time, while you start again.

Good to know
Diamond plates and doors are one colour, squares the other. Shape tells them apart.
Bodies never share a tile. A blocked ghost just waits that turn and carries on.
⏳Hit the turn limit and the loop rewinds itself. Run out of loops and the level is lost — restart and try a cleverer plan.
🏆A ghost on the exit does not count. You have to stand there.
Keys

← ↓ ↑ → or WASD move · Space wait · Enter rewind · Backspace restart · Esc pause. Every one of them is a button on screen too, and you can swipe the board.
