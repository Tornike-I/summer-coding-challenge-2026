Bloom

Plant a few seeds, press Grow, and hope the garden thinks its way onto every bud.

Conway’s Game of Life, as puzzle golf. Six gardens.

How to play

Two rules run the whole garden

Birth. An empty cell with exactly three living neighbours comes alive.

Survival. A living cell with two or three living neighbours lives on.

Everything else dies. Too lonely or too crowded, and the cell goes dark. All eight neighbours count; outside the garden is always dead.

Your turn is only the planting

Click, tap or drag on the garden to place seeds — a level gives you a limited number. Stone cells cannot be seeded, though life still grows across them. Then press Grow and watch.

You win the moment one grown generation has every bud alive at the same time — seeding the buds themselves does not count, the garden has to get there. Extra life elsewhere is fine. Reset puts the garden back to your planting with the seeds intact, so you can nudge one seed and try again.
