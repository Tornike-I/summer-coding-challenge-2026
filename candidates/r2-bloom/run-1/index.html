Bloom

Plant a few seeds, press Grow, and let Conway’s Life carry them to every bud at once.

 

How to play

Two rules run the whole garden. Every cell counts its eight neighbours.

Three neighbours → a birth

An empty cell with exactly three live neighbours comes to life.

Two or three → it lives

A live cell with two or three live neighbours survives the generation.

Anything else → it dies

Alone or crowded, the cell dies. Nothing lives outside the garden wall.

The game

Each level marks a few buds and gives you a small handful of seeds. Plant them, press Grow, and you win the moment a single generation has every bud alive at the same time. The count starts at generation 1, so planting straight onto the buds is never enough. Extra life elsewhere is fine.

Stone is bare rock: you can never plant on it, but life spreads across it exactly as it does anywhere else.

Reset puts the garden back to your planting with the seeds still in place, so you can move one cell instead of starting over. A Hint reveals one cell of a known solution and costs 5 score.

Controls

  • ←↑↓→ move the cursor
  • Enter Space plant or lift a seed
  • G grow / pause
  • S one step
  • R reset to your planting
  • H hint
  • Esc pause / back
  • Drag with a finger or mouse to plant several
