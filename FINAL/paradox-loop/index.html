Paradox Loop

The room needs more hands than you have. Rewind, and borrow your own past.

Arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen buttons.

How to play

1

Move, one turn at a time

One key press is one turn. Walls, shut doors and other bodies refuse you — a refused move costs nothing.

2

Plates hold doors open

A door stays open while a body stands on a plate of its colour. Round plates open round doors, diamond opens diamond.

3

Rewind and meet yourself

Rewind sends the room back to the start. A ghost now replays your last attempt beside you — standing on that plate so you can walk through.

Keys

  • ←↑↓→ or WASD move — Space wait
  • Enter rewind — Backspace restart level — Esc pause
  • On a touch screen, swipe the board or use the buttons.
