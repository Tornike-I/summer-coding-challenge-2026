An abstract puzzle about shapes on a grid.

How to play

1

Fill one district at a time. Click or drag on empty cells. The first cell starts the district; every cell after it must touch the district you are drawing, so it can never break apart.

2

Every district is the same size. When it is full it locks, and the next one starts on its own. A cell that is not touching the district you are drawing is refused — nothing breaks.

3

Seats, not voters. Whoever holds more cells in a district takes that seat; an even split is a tie and counts for nobody. Teal passes the level by holding more seats than Amber once every cell is assigned. In the grid here Teal has 4 voters out of 9 — and 2 seats out of 3.
