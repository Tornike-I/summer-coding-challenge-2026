Bloom

Plant a few seeds, then let Life do the rest.

Conway's Game of Life, played as puzzle golf.

How to play

Two rules, run on every cell at once.

BornA dead cell with exactly 3 live neighbours comes alive.
SurvivesA live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours stays alive.
DiesAnything else dies — too lonely, or too crowded.

The game

Plant up to the level's seed allowance wherever you like, then press Grow. You win the moment one generation has every bud alive at the same time. Extra life elsewhere is fine. You never touch the garden once it grows — Reset puts it back to your planting, seeds intact.

The garden

  • A seed you planted, waiting to grow.
  • A living cell.
  • A bud, still dark — an empty ring.
  • A lit bud — the ring holds a diamond.
  • Stone: no seed can be planted here, but life still grows across it.

Controls

Click, tap or drag to plant. Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Enter or Space plants, G grows, S steps one generation, R resets to your planting, Esc pauses. Everything outside the garden wall is permanently dead.

Levels

Best score is seeds used, then generations taken. Hints cost 2 seeds each.
